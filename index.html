01 — Floor systems

Four cores. One 600 mm grid.

Every system sits on height-adjustable pedestals and lifts out panel by panel. What changes is the core — and with it the load, the acoustics and the finish.

02 — Anatomy

Six layers, each doing one job.

A steel cementitious anti-static panel, taken apart. Hover a layer to see what it contributes.

  1. Anti-static veneerHPL or conductive PVC wearing surface. Bleeds charge away in a controlled 10⁶–10¹⁰ Ω range.
  2. Conductive strakeBlack conductive edge trim on all four sides. Protects the veneer edge and bonds panel to panel.
  3. Steel plate surfaceFlat top sheet, epoxy coated. Flatness held to ≤ 0.6 mm across the panel.
  4. Cement-filling layerLightweight cement infill. Adds mass and stiffness, deadens footfall, will not burn.
  5. Steel plate bottomDeep-drawn dimpled pan, welded to the top sheet so the two act as one structure.
  6. Pedestal & stringerHeight-adjustable supports at all four corners, tied together with galvanised steel beams.

05 — Accessories & other products

Everything the floor needs to be finished.

Support systems and accessories are produced in-house, so they adjust to the panel, the height and the installation method you choose.

Ventilated floor panels

All-steel panels with punched vents through the top sheet, bottom sheet and veneer. Drop them in wherever the room needs air delivered from under the floor.

Interchangeable with FS panels · multiple perforation patterns

Hollow panel

Light all-steel panel on a 500 mm grid for general office floors.

Raceway & trunking panels

FS-series bare panels with integrated cable raceway, for under-carpet office wiring.

FS 800–2500 · 600 × 600 mm

Floor beams

Galvanised steel stringers that lock the pedestal grid together; buffer tape applied where the project calls for it.

Supporting brackets

Height-adjustable pedestals matched to each panel specification and installation method.

Floor lifting machine

Single- and double-sucker panel lifters for installation and day-to-day maintenance.

Outlet boxes & grounding sockets

Panels are tapped on site or delivered pre-tapped, then fitted with outlet boxes and special grounding sockets developed for raised floors.

Anti-static melamine cover
