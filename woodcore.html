Overview

Woodcore panels are made of high-density composite wood encapsulated in steel sheets. They are highly durable and reliable, and show good acoustic performance.

With an anti-static HPL or PVC top surface they suit offices, control rooms and classrooms — abrasion-proof, corrosion-free and an environmentally friendly product.

  • Good acoustic performance
  • Abrasion-proof
  • Corrosion-free
  • Environmentally friendly
  • Absorbs indoor noise
  • Easy to clean
