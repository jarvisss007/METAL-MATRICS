Overview

Aluminium access floors are cast from aluminium ingot. They are lightweight, non-corrosive and sturdy, and show good electrical and thermal conductivity.

They are widely used in data centres, aerospace facilities, military command headquarters and clean rooms. The advantage of these floors is that they are economical and fully recyclable.

  • Lightweight
  • Non-corrosive
  • Sturdy
  • Electrically conductive
  • Thermally conductive
  • Fully recyclable
