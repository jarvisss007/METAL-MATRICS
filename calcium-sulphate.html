Calcium Sulphate Raised Access Floor Panels

High-density calcium sulphate encased in hot-dip galvanised steel. The mineral core takes the impact; the steel takes the bending.

Overview

Calcium sulphate panels are among the most widely used raised floors. Each panel is a high-density calcium sulphate core encased in galvanised formed steel sheet, with top and bottom sheets locked together through profiled edges for extra robustness.

The result is a floor with high dimensional precision that is anti-static, shock-proof, fire-proof and pollution-free — quick to assemble, on pedestals whose height can be adjusted at all four corners.

  • Fire-proof
  • Anti-static
  • High dimensional precision
  • Shock-proof
  • Pollution-free
  • Quick to assemble
  • Adjustable pedestal height

Construction

How the panel is built

Black conductive PVC trim
Edge protection on all four sides that also bonds panel to panel.
Calcium sulphate core
High-density mineral core: resists high temperature and will not burn.
Galvanised sheet base
Formed steel sheet that carries the bending load and resists corrosion.
Corner supports
Height-adjustable pedestal at each of the four corners.
