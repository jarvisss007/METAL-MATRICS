Steel Cementitious Raised Access Floor Panels

A welded steel shell filled with lightweight cement. Six grades, from open offices to heavy equipment halls.

Request a quoteSpec sheet

Overview

The panel is made of steel plate infilled with light cement, and the surface is coated with epoxy paint. A screw-down design supports the system effectively, so installation is easy and the structure is stable.

The lightweight cement makes the panel strong and deadens noise. Steel cementitious panels are the standard choice for communication centres, data centres and computer rooms.

  • Non-combustible
  • Non-toxic
  • Silent underfoot
  • Stable across temperature
  • Screw-down fixing
  • Six load grades

Construction

How the panel is built

Anti-static veneer
HPL or PVC wearing surface with controlled electrical resistance.
Conductive strake
Conductive edge trim around the veneer.
Steel plate surface
Flat, epoxy-coated top sheet.
Cement-filling layer
Lightweight cement infill for mass, stiffness and quiet.
Steel plate bottom
Deep-drawn dimpled pan welded to the top sheet.
